Carbon and Capital Demo Privacy Notice

Who I am

Carbon and Capital is made by Sava Cristian Urigiuc. I am a solo developer based in the United Kingdom. I am also the data controller for the information described in this notice.

Contact: carbonandcapital.game@gmail.com

What this notice covers

This notice covers the gameplay data the free demo of Carbon and Capital sends to me while "Send data to developer" is switched on. It applies to the demo distributed through Steam and itch.io.

Steam and itch.io collect their own data (your account, downloads, achievements, store activity) under their own privacy policies. I do not receive that data and this notice does not cover it.

What the demo sends

At the end of each run, the demo sends me:

  • A random player ID, generated on your device the first time the demo runs. It is not linked to your name, email, or Steam or itch.io account. You can see it in Settings.
  • Your operating system family (for example "Windows") and the demo version.
  • A random run ID and session ID, and the times the run started, ended and was sent.
  • Game state for each turn: money, buildings, production, costs, revenue, profit, loans, power, transport and progress towards victory.
  • Which in-game panels and encyclopedia pages you opened, and on which turn.
  • How the run ended (for example closing the window), total playtime, rescues used, whether cheats were used, and tutorial progress.

What is not collected

The demo does not send your name, email address, username, or any Steam or itch.io account details. I do not receive your IP address: the data arrives through a Google service that does not show me where it came from.

Nothing about your hardware beyond the operating system family, no location, no files from your computer, and no text you type.

Why I collect it

I use the data to balance the game, find bugs, and see where players get stuck or stop playing. It is used only for statistics about how the demo is played. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never combined with other information about you.

Under UK law, statistical information about how a game is used may be collected without consent if you are told clearly what is collected and can switch it off easily (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, as amended by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025). Under UK GDPR my lawful basis is legitimate interests: improving the demo, weighed against the low sensitivity of the data. The same switch and the same rights apply wherever you live.

Where the data goes

The demo sends the data over an encrypted connection to a Google Sheets spreadsheet in my Google account. Only I can see it. Google stores it on my behalf as a service provider under its own data protection terms, and may store it outside the UK.

I do not share the data with anyone else and I do not use any third-party analytics service. I may publish aggregate figures, such as how many players reach a given turn, that cannot identify anyone.

How long I keep it

I delete the raw per-run data no later than 6 months after it is received, and currently aim for 3 months. Aggregate statistics that cannot identify anyone may be kept longer.

If you ask me to delete your data, I delete every row matching your player ID.

Turning it off

The first time you run the demo you will see a short screen explaining this data and where the switch is. The switch is "Send data to developer" in Settings. Turn it off and nothing further is sent; you can turn it back on at any time.

Turning it off does not delete data already sent. To do that, email me as described below.

Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of the data linked to your player ID, ask me to correct or delete it, or object to my using it. Because I hold no name or contact details, I can only find your data if you tell me your player ID from Settings. Email carbonandcapital.game@gmail.com and I will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or, if you live outside the UK, to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this notice

This is version 1.0 of the Privacy Policy, dated 18 October 2026.

I will update this notice before any major change to what the demo sends, where it goes, or how it is analysed, including adding any new analytics system. Each update changes the version number and date above, and significant changes will be mentioned on the demo's store page.
